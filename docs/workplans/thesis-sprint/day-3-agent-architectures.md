## Objective

Implement the later architecture comparison for treatments B0, B1, and A1
against the same accepted replay inputs and metric decision kernel. Treatment
identities, prompts or deterministic policies, capability access, evidence,
and stopping conditions must remain distinct and reviewable.

## Queued boundary

No B0, B1, or A1 architecture is implemented or evaluated during Day 1.

time availability, and effect-free outputs. Any LLM use, if later authorized
by the accepted Day 3 plan, must remain isolated from provider, broker, and
portfolio mutation effects and must disclose its limitations.
