# Capability taxonomy

Initial families:

- acquisition: retrieve eligible data through a governed source;
- preparation: map, normalize, validate and bind typed inputs;
- analytics: calculate deterministic portfolio and risk measures;
- quantitative model: execute ML, EVT, Monte Carlo, diffusion or related models;
- interpretation: transform validated evidence into bounded explanatory work;
- presentation: render tables, charts, reports and dashboard components;
- governance: validate evidence, temporal eligibility, policy and effects;
- meta-capability: validate, document, fixture-test or propose changes to system objects.

Atomic capabilities remain independently versioned. User-facing packages group
projected capability tools without changing their individual contracts.
